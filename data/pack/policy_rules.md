# HR Policy Rules — Emirates Group (synthetic pack)

This file is generated from the compiled rule pack (PACK_VERSION emirates-hr-1.0.0). The verbatim text below is the source of record for each rule.

## R01 · Exit approvals: line manager and HR business partner

Every exit transaction must be approved by the employee's line manager and an HR business partner.

## R02 · Approval recorded after submission

Approval must be recorded after the transaction is submitted.

## R03 · Transfer approvals: sending and receiving managers

A transfer must be approved by the employee's sending manager (their line manager before the transfer).
A transfer must also be approved by the receiving department manager.

## R04 · Allowance changes above AED 10,000 need a grade 13+ approver

Any allowance change above AED 10,000 must be approved by someone at grade 13 or above.

## R05 · Pay changes above AED 25,000 need a grade 15+ approver

Any pay change above AED 25,000 must be approved by someone at grade 15 or above.

## R06 · Segregation of duties: requester and beneficiary cannot approve

The requester of a transaction cannot approve it.
No one may approve a transaction from which they benefit.

## R07 · New hire documents

Every new hire must have a signed offer letter and a passport copy attached; non-national hires must also have a visa copy.

## R08 · No backdating beyond 30 days

A transaction must not be backdated by more than 30 days.

## R09 · Pay and allowance changes only for active employees

No pay or allowance change may be processed for an employee who is not active on the effective date.

## R10 · At most two allowance changes in 12 months

No employee may receive more than two allowance changes in any 12-month period.

## R11 · No duplicate allowance changes within 3 days

The same allowance change for the same employee and the same amount must not be entered twice within 3 days.

## R12 · Approvers exist and are active

Every approver must exist in the employee master and be active at the time of approval.

## R13 · Pay increases above 15% need grade 17 approval

A pay increase of more than 15% is not permitted without Grade 17 approval.

## R14 · Grade ladder definition

For this policy, grade 20 is the most senior grade and grade 1 is the most junior; grades 13 and above are considered senior leadership.

## R15 · Transfer justification (human attestation)

For each transfer, the receiving manager must provide reasonable justification for the business need.

## Scope note

All rules apply to transactions with an effective date on or after 1 January 2025.
