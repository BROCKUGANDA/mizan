# MIZAN pilot plan — 12 weeks

Roles: HR compliance lead (owner), HRIS data steward (data feeds), IT platform
owner (operations), internal audit (read-only). Weekly metrics reviewed Fridays.

| Week | Phase | Activities | Exit metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Onboard | Analyst walkthrough (20 min, EN/AR), rule-owner guide, runbooks RB-01..08 rehearsal, on-call rota agreed | All analysts completed walkthrough |
| 2 | Baseline | Load 12 months, pin backtest baselines, agree decision SLAs, DPIA finalised | Baseline report signed |
| 3–6 | Shadow | O1 shadow profile: findings stored, nothing routes; measure precision on decided exceptions; tune mapping (not rules) | Measured precision ≥ 90% (Wilson LB) on decided exceptions |
| 7–8 | Assisted | Routing on for Low/Medium; digest cadence agreed; noise ratio watched | Noise ratio ≤ 10%; no rule > 20% auto-flagged |
| 9–10 | Full | All severities route; escalation ladder live; audit room handed to internal audit | Decision coverage 100%; deadline adherence ≥ 90% |
| 11 | Harden | Fix-stickiness review, recurrence tracking, runbook updates | Fix stickiness improving month over month |
| 12 | Go/no-go | Readiness score ≥ 80, red lines verified, expansion plan (second process/entity) | Go decision + one expansion named |

Weekly metrics (PRD §6.3, instrumented via product events): analyst week-4
retention ≥ 90%; team-lead retention ≥ 75%; stickiness ≥ 0.6; time-to-first
action ≤ 1 business day; noise ratio ≤ 10%; deadline adherence ≥ 90%;
sampling effort avoided ≥ 50%.
