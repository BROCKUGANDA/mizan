# RB-02 — Flood guard hold

Steps: detect → contain → recover → verify → communicate (edge-case doc §9).

Detect 'R0x held' alert. Contain: automatic per-rule routing pause. Recover: inspect 20 held; fix mapping and re-run, or compliance lead releases with reason. Verify: fail rate back in band. Communicate: tell owner teams exceptions are delayed.

Rehearsed: before submission and in pilot week 1.
