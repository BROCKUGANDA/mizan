The problem

Sampling audits miss what matters

1 in 20

A 5% sample finds roughly one in twenty problems. The other nineteen approve pay, move grades and exit people anyway.

After the fact

Quarterly review reports on a period that already closed. The overpayment is recovered, if at all, months later.

No audit trail

Spreadsheets of findings cannot show who decided what, on which evidence, under whose authority.

MIZAN checks every transaction against every written clause on every run — in seconds — and turns each failure into an exception that explains itself and waits for a person.

Every flag argues for itself

What broke

Employee E04567 had an exit date of 2026-02-28, but transaction WX-1003 changed pay by AED 8,200.00 with an effective date of 2026-03-10.

The rule (verbatim)

No pay or allowance change may be processed for an employee who is not active on the effective date.

Evidence
amount_filsAED 8,200.00transaction
exit_dateexit 2026-02-28employee_master
status at effective dateACTIVE → EXITEDemployee_master
Why it matters · next action · routing · severity rationale

Overpayments to leavers are hard to recover → hold payment, confirm status, recover under policy → routed by routing-1.2.0 → CRITICAL because pay eligibility is Critical by policy.

  • Eight parts on every finding: headline, what broke, verbatim rule, evidence, why it matters, next action, owner and severity, caveats
  • Every evidence value names the record it came from; breaching values are highlighted
  • Severity is a base plus at most two stated modifier steps — the rationale is shown, not implied
  • Routing resolves owner by override → routing table → family overlay, then a conflict-of-interest guard refuses to hand it to a party
  • English and Arabic, RTL first-class, with SLA due dates from the versioned UAE calendar

06
Responsible AI and security — 20%

The engine decides. A person closes.

Model optional, fact-locked

The gateway only rewrites wording. Every number, date and ID must come from the evidence; accusatory phrasing is rejected; any failure falls back to the approved template. ai.mode=none is the default and the demo runs that way.

Human decision on every exception

Nothing auto-closes. Dismissing a High or Critical finding requires a second analyst's countersign — self-countersigning is refused by the API, as is deciding a case you requested, approved or benefit from.

Audit trail that can be verified

Append-only, hash-chained: every run, decision, countersign, rule change and export. Tampering fails verification and drives the system into safe halt. mizan audit verify reproduces the head hash offline.

Data handling

Synthetic data only; a PII scanner blocks real identifiers at upload. Uploads are size-, line- and row-limited with MIME sniffing. Exports escape formula injection. No write credentials exist for any source system.

Pattern alerts are advisory only — never routed, never exported, never scored against a person. Small demographic cells (<5) are suppressed. Full statement: docs/RAI_STATEMENT.md.

Deployability and reuse — 15%

Adding a rule is typing a sentence

# Rule studio — plain English, no model required
Any allowance change above AED 10,000 must be
approved by someone at grade 13 or above.

→ comparator >   → quantifier any   (ambiguity chips, not guesses)
→ 3 boundary tests generated
→ backtest 1,061 txns in 0.42 s: would flag 3 · 0.3%
→ overlap: R04, Jaccard 1.0  — it is already policy

Two-person approval, then a shadow run, before a rule can go active. Every activation is an immutable version with its own hash.

  • Everything that varies is versioned data: routing tables, UAE business calendar, grade ladder, synonyms, severity rubric, SLA policy, action library — editable without a deploy
  • A second business unit loads its own rule pack; packs export and import as YAML with tests and a canonical hash
  • Effective-dated weekend change (Fri–Sat → Sat–Sun, 2022) and provisional public holidays are handled as data with caveats
  • Bilingual EN/AR with RTL layout and bidirectional isolates — built for Emirates and reusable across government entities
  • Runs fully offline: no CDN, no telemetry, bundled fonts and icons

The ask

Give MIZAN one HR process for a quarter

Pick the noisiest process — allowance changes or exits — and run twelve weeks in shadow against its real transaction history. The measure is not how many findings MIZAN produces; it is decision coverage at 100%, noise under 10% and deadlines met nine times in ten, with every one of them traceable to a rule, a record and a named person.
